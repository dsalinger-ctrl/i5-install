DAILY SOP  
STANDARD ONSITE DAY-  
MOBILIZATION:

* Meet at Vibe Board. Quickly review teams on trello and break off for [drawing reviews](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1UEuYfUZHKfia7xOfdBRzLUmxa3y8CAUlKOjHYPfezEI/edit?tab=t.0). {2 minutes}  
* [Drawing reviews](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1UEuYfUZHKfia7xOfdBRzLUmxa3y8CAUlKOjHYPfezEI/edit?tab=t.0) in assigned teams. {10 minutes}  
* Re-assemble at vibe board. Each team communicates with lead to clarify tasks. {5 \- 10 MINUTES}  
* Fill out Safety assessment in Procore.    
* Mobilize- {10 minutes \- 1 hour}   
  * Unload and stage all items listed in *Mobilize* card.    
  * Setup work areas, carts, and lift bins.  
  * Safely CRANK on assigned tasks.

THE DAY:

* Lead inspects decor layout before drilling, structural attachments, electrical connections, and decor plumb/level.  
* Each time the team moves to a new work area, fill out Safety Assessmen[t](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1Q8dc4jJdcflwl4vbtAPJeDy4lUGKtuW12qgneHk4EN4/edit) form.  
* Radio lead 15 minutes before finishing a task so he can prepare the next area/objective for your team OR with impediments preventing you from finishing, redirect if needed.  
* Meet every 2 \- 3 hours at Vibe Board to check off completed work on trello, review next objectives.  
* Lunch Break-  
  * Lead radios 15 minutes before lunch break.   
  * Teams come to a safe stopping point within 10 minutes.  
  * Remove debris/organize work areas for 5 minutes.   
  * The entire team leaves the site together.  
  * Returning from lunch- team assembles outside, walks in together to meet at Vibe Board.  

END OF DAY:

* Lead radios 15 minutes before meeting at vibe board.  
* Everyone comes to a safe stopping point, set up to continue cranking the following day. Review supplies, tools, or rentals needed to complete the decor. {10 minutes}  
* Take one close “detail” photo of your work from the day. Examples include structural connections, inside of power supply box you wired, or bolted seam you completed.  
* Clean work areas {5 minutes}.  
  * Organize your work carts.  
  * Put away your personal tools, bring company tools to job box.  
  * Drive lifts to designated charging area.  
  * Ladders and work carts to staging area.  
  * Dispose of all trash into trash bins.   
* Meet at vibe board, update trello with current status and discuss any items that need ordered or picked up and zoom install manager on *Installers* chat for approval. {8 minutes}  
* Each team member cranks on assigned EOD tasks, take photos. {15 minutes}  
* Lead confirms with install manager and zooms start time and first task for every team member on *Installers* chat.  
* Once all assignments complete, the exterior and interior teams conduct their separate obvious walks. We want to catch anything that would reflect badly on our team with our customers and other contractors and set ourselves up for success the following day. Fix any issue the team sees in each area. {10 minutes}  
* Everyone assembles at the vehicles, reads and likes the zoom message for the following day.  
* Drive home. Non-drivers send EOD email to install manager and Mr. O’Connor.  
* EOD emails should be specific to your tasks of the day. The whole team hangs a large structure, but there are multiple roles within that- list your specific part. Also, if you’d like to communicate any issues with loading, crating, pre-assembly, or anything else feel free. Anything that can help the team work more efficiently and safely is appreciated.  
  Good example for EOD email:  
* *D13 lifted and leveled. Mike confirmed level.*  
  * *Spotted for DeMarco unloading décor crates.*  
  * *Installed eyebolts and vacuumed out D13 (photo attached).*  
  * *Manned a material lift during décor lift.*  
* *Secured trailer and surrounding area (photos attached).*  
* *Area was very congested today with contractors and supplies. I suggested to Mike that we remove excess pallets out of our way, and he talked to GC about it. Nothing was moved by EOD.*  
* Once you’ve sent your EOD email, punch out and get a good rest.

THURSDAY EACH WEEK- Begin the day with 1 hour of online safety training.

FRIDAY EACH WEEK- Walkthrough for following week’s installations.

LAST ONSITE DAY- Demobilize replaces EOD:

* Remove all install materials and tools, prepare featherlite for travel. (Team Review)  
* Call off rental equipment.  
* Detail clean the entire jobsite for Matterport Scans.  
* Matterport scan.  
* Empty and call off trailers.  
* Organize trailers and storage containers remaining on site and update collaboration sheet.  
* Obvious Walk the entire project including finished phases and exterior staging area to take care of any “Spaghetti Stains”.  
* Lead has final walkthrough with site super, team addresses any issues.

INSTALL WALKTHROUGH-

* Look at published design renderings to see finished product.  
* Briefly review each decor item.  
* Scope and schedule overview.  
* View latest Matterport scan.  
* Assign each team member tasks for first day of install and EOD procedure.  
* Review site specific safety protocol, badging process, etc.  
* Google Earth and General Sheets to see unloading pathways.    
* Discuss and update trello.

AIR TRAVEL TO PROJECT-

* Whenever possible, combine rides to the airport. Avoid 7 different Ubers.  
* Clock in when you leave your house. If the team is going to project site directly from the airport, change into your uniform before final flight.  
* First person zooms “At my gate” on *Installers* chat once they’ve arrived.   
* Each team member likes the message when they arrive.  
* Lead zooms “Landed” on *Installers* chat when you land.  
* Lead zooms “Onsite” on *Installers* chat once the team has arrived to project.   
* One person from each rental house zooms confirmation they’ve arrived and the house is in good condition.  
* Take photos of each room of the house and upload to Procore photo album.  
* Lead zooms on *Installers* chat start time and first task for every team member.  
* Each team member reads and likes the message.  
* Clock out and get a good rest.

DRIVING TRAVEL TO PROJECT-

* Fill out a Pre-travel checklist prior to leaving each day.  
* Zoom on *Installers* chat when you are on the road and finished for the day.  
* Drive 8 hours per day. Communicate with the install manager if more hours are needed to stay on schedule.   
* On evening before last day of driving, zoom estimated arrival time on *Installers* so the project team can plan for your arrival.
